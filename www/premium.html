Location Services Disabled

To show you nearby deals, Glass Card needs access to your location.

Android: Open your device's Settings, go to Location > App location permissions, tap Glass Card, and choose your preferred access level.
iOS: Open your Settings app, tap Privacy & Security, and select Location Services. Ensure the master toggle at the top is turned on, then scroll down, tap Glass Card, and choose your preferred access level.
